Questions

Common questions

Do you repair gutters?

No. We clear and check them. If we see a cracked joint or a loose bracket we tell you, with a photo, so you can get it fixed by a roofer.

Which areas do you cover?

Send your postcode in the form. We will tell you straight away if you are outside the area we can reach.

How often should gutters be cleared?

Most houses need it once a year, more often near tall trees. We will not push a plan you do not need.

Gutterlark clears and inspects gutters only. We do not carry out roofing, gutter replacement or repairs at height.
